# Qollqa Pay refund policy (fictional, demo only)

Qollqa Pay is a fictional wallet. This policy is for the demo. It is not a real policy.

1. You can ask for a refund of a completed purchase, bill or subscription payment.
2. You must ask within 30 days of the payment date.
3. The refund returns the payment amount. The Qollqa Pay fee is not returned.
4. A payment that failed was not charged. There is nothing to refund.
5. A pending payment cannot be refunded yet. Wait until it is completed.
6. A payment that is already refunded cannot be refunded again.
7. A payment with an open dispute cannot be refunded. The dispute team decides.
8. A transfer to a person cannot be refunded here. Ask the receiver, or open a dispute.
9. Every refund request needs your confirmation. The assistant can only make a draft.
10. If you do not recognize a charge, do not ask for a refund. Open a dispute: a person checks it.
